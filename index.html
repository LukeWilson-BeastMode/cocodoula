About

Birth asks a lot of you. You should not go through it alone.

I’m Coco, a trained birth doula in Austin, Texas, and a mother of four. Birth can be exciting, emotional, and full of unknowns, all at once. More than anything, I see my role as an advocate for mothers during some of their most vulnerable moments.

Every birth is different. My job is to know you, your hopes, and your preferences well enough that when the day comes, we step into it together - not as strangers to the moment, but as a team that already knows what matters.

Coco

How the care works

Before

Prenatal visits in your home. We build your preferences, talk through what you want and what you are afraid of, and practice comfort measures with your partner so they know exactly what to do.

During

On call from 37 weeks. I come when you want me and I stay until after the baby is born. Continuous physical comfort, steady information, and a voice in the room that is only yours.

After

A postpartum visit in the first week and open texts beyond it. Feeding, healing, sleep, and the honest conversation about how you are actually doing.
